Hanging

Under the shelf, unloaded and loaded

The hanger shaft turns freely in the shelf's hole, and the chassis swings on the hinge pin under it. Together they let the reel face and lean toward any pull, so the shaft is never bent. A load hanging straight down tips the reel 30°, which puts the line right under the hinge pin.

Inside

Wheel, lock, and hinge

The wheel is an open cup. The line goes through a small hole in the groove floor and is knotted inside. The lock pin passes through both cheeks and one hole in the wheel's 5 mm web. The lock and hinge holes are 8.4 mm, so a round pencil crayon can stand in for either pin.

On the bed

Every part as it prints

The only overhang wider than 45° is the flat underside of the bridge, which spans 28 mm between the cheeks. The chassis's horizontal holes have pointed tops. The shaft prints on its side, so its layers run the length of the pull.
